About

A developer who ships real products

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before starting your project.

What services do you offer?

I build business websites, custom web applications and dashboards, e-commerce stores and point-of-sale systems, and landing pages and portfolios. Every project includes design, development, and deployment.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of your project — a landing page costs far less than a full web application with user accounts and an admin panel. Email me with your idea for honest advice, a clear plan, and a fair price.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page can launch in days. A business website typically takes one to three weeks, while a custom web application with dashboards and integrations takes longer depending on features. You get a clear timeline before work starts.

Which technologies do you use?

Modern, proven tools: React and Next.js with TypeScript and Tailwind CSS on the frontend; Node.js and FastAPI backends; MongoDB, PostgreSQL and Prisma for data; and progressive web app (PWA) techniques for fast, installable experiences.

Do you work with clients outside Jordan?

Yes. I am based in Irbid, Jordan and I work with clients worldwide — everything from planning to launch happens online, in English or Arabic.

How do I start a project with you?

Email me describing your project. You will get a reply with honest advice, a clear plan, and a fair price — no pressure and no jargon.
